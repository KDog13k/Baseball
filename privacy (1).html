Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BaseWits ("we," "our," or "the Site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit basewits.com and play the BaseWits trivia game.

Information We Collect

BaseWits does not require account creation, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or other direct personal identifying information to play the game.

We do use third-party services that automatically collect limited technical information, described below.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are visited, how long people play, and general device/location information (such as country or city-level location, browser type, and device type). This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this data. You can learn more about how Google handles this information at policies.google.com/privacy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising

BaseWits may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site or other sites on the internet.

Local Storage

BaseWits stores certain gameplay information directly on your own device using your browser's local storage, including your high score, career statistics, achievements, and whether you've played that day's game. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. BaseWits itself does not set cookies directly, but the third-party services described above (Google Analytics and, if applicable, Google AdSense) do use cookies as part of their normal operation. You can control or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time.

Children's Privacy

BaseWits is a general-audience trivia game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below so we can address it.

Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites, and we encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information associated with the Site, including restricting how our trivia question data can be accessed. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of those changes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at contact@basewits.com.

This policy is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice.
